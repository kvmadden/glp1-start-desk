| Mounjaro (tirzepatide) | [Prescribing information, revised 11/2026](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0) | 2.1: 2.5 mg, then 2.5 mg steps after at least 4 weeks each, maximum 15 mg in adults. Missed dose within 4 days, otherwise skip. Same contraceptive advice as Zepbound |

From the warnings sections of all four labels: stop if pancreatitis is suspected, gallbladder studies if gallstones are suspected, monitor kidney function when stomach effects could cause fluid loss, consider lowering insulin or a sulfonylurea for low sugars, and tell the care team before any procedure under sedation. The boxed warning makes a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2 a contraindication. Pregnancy: Wegovy and Zepbound say to stop when pregnancy is recognized, and the semaglutide labels say to stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy.

Only the Wegovy label has a restart rule after missed doses. For the other three, the desk asks the prescriber which dose to restart at.

## Medicare

- Part D excludes drugs when used for weight loss: Social Security Act 1860D-2(e)(2), which points to [section 1927(d)(2)(A)](https://www.ssa.gov/OP_Home/ssact/title19/1927.htm). The desk flags Medicare patients whose only listed use is weight. Heart risk (Wegovy) and sleep apnea (Zepbound) are separate indications.
- [Medicare GLP-1 Bridge](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/prescription-drug-coverage/medicare-glp-1-bridge), CMS page modified July 13, 2026: a demonstration outside the Part D benefit, July 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027, with a $50 copay for those who qualify.

## Planning rules (not from a label)

- A prescriber answers a fax within 2 business days, so a step request leaves 3 calendar days before the supply runs out.
- A check-in a few days after each new strength, and in the first week.
- Prior authorization, savings card and stock checks are pharmacy operations, not clinical rules.
